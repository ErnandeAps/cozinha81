## Quick orientation
- **Brand:** Ink `#1A1A1A` + Flame `#C4520A` (the one lit burner). Warm "steel" neutrals — never blue-gray. Flame used surgically.
- **Type:** Archivo (display/headings, 800–900, tight), Hanken Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (labels/prices/`//` eyebrows). Loaded from Google Fonts.
- **Motif:** a 4-burner cooktop with exactly one burner lit in flame. Reused as loader, hero graphic, station tiles, bullets.
- **Voice:** Brazilian Portuguese, direct and capable, no hype, no emoji. "você" for the customer, "nós" own the boring parts.
- **Components:** link `styles.css`, load `_ds_bundle.js`, read primitives from `window.Cozinha81DesignSystem_72690b`.
